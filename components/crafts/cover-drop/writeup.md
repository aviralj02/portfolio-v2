A cover picker where you drag a photo onto the cover.

Wanted an excuse to try Three.js, so the dropped photo lands on a gridded plane instead of an image tag. A small shader sends rings out from the centre that lift the surface and catch the light, while the photo spreads out and comes into colour. Once the waves settle, a plain image takes over.

The slot keeps a faint imprint of the photo, and removing the cover drains it back to the centre as a droplet springs home.
