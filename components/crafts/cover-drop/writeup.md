A cover picker where you drag a photo from the column onto the cover.

Pick up a print and it lifts and tilts a little. While you hold it over the cover, the drop zone swells and asks you to let go. Drop it anywhere outside and it springs back to its slot.

On a drop, the photo spreads into the cover like a drop of ink on water. A soft-edged circle grows out from the centre past the corners, and the photo is drawn on a fine 3D surface whose waves rise and catch the light as they roll outward, then go still. The image floats outward as it spreads, pale and soft at first, then settles into full colour.

Remove it and the drop runs in reverse, all at once. The cover drains back into its centre through the same soft circle, while a small droplet springs home and spreads back out into its slot. Drop a new photo on a filled cover and the old one goes home while the new one lands. You can also click a photo to set it without dragging.
