A small icon tab bar where only the active tab gets a name.

Pick a tab and the pill stretches toward it before it lets go of the old one. Its two edges run on different curves: the leading edge shoots across with a strong ease-out, while the trailing edge starts slow, holds the old tab for a beat, then snaps over. No timers, just two easings.

Hover a tab and the pill leans a few pixels toward it, like it already knows where you're going. The new icon fills in solid and its label fades in with a small slide from the side the pill came from, then rests in place while the tabs make room.

The unread badge rides on the inbox icon and flips its colors under the pill. Arrow keys move between tabs, and slow motion stretches every timing so you can watch the edges separate.
